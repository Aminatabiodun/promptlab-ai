THE BASICS

Build a prompt in layers

01

Subject

What should appear? Be specific about the person, object, animal, or scene.

02

Context

Describe where it is, the time of day, weather, mood, and surroundings.

03

Style

Choose a visual direction such as 3D, cinematic, watercolor, or photorealistic.

04

Technical details

Add lighting, camera angle, composition, depth of field, colors and quality cues.
